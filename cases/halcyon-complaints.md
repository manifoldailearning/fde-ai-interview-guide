# Halcyon Bank: resolving complaints faster

## Prompt (read aloud)

Halcyon Bank receives customer complaints by email and web form.
Regulators expect complaints to be acknowledged and resolved
within set time limits. The head of customer operations wants AI
to cut the time it takes to resolve complaints. Structure the
problem.

## Facts to reveal only when asked

- **Volume.** About 3,000 complaints a month: about a third card
  disputes, a quarter fees, the rest mixed.
- **Work.** Handlers read the complaint, search several systems
  for the customer's history, decide, and write a final response
  letter that must include specific regulatory wording.
- **Time.** Most elapsed time is waiting: for information from
  other departments, for approval of refunds above a threshold,
  and in a queue before a handler picks the complaint up.
- **Quality.** The quality team samples letters for errors.

## Change of facts (minute 20)

"The risk committee has just ruled that no customer data may
leave the country, and no external model provider is approved
yet."

## Watch for

- Asks how much of the elapsed time is active work versus waiting.
- Splits the plan: process fixes for waiting, AI for work.
- Regulatory wording inserted from approved templates by code.
- Pilot on card disputes; handler minutes per complaint primary,
  total resolution time reported; letter errors and time-limit
  breaches as guardrails.
- After the change: in-country hosting, approval on the critical
  path, model-free work started now, evaluation before the pilot,
  wedge unchanged.

## Common weak moves

- Assumes faster letter drafting will shorten resolution time.
- Lets the model write regulatory wording.
- Restarts or abandons the plan after the change of facts.
